Approach
01

Positioning

Before any district or project, we define what this money is for: how long it can be away, what liquidity you need, and whether the goal is capital preservation, rental income or growth. The investor is analysed before the asset.

02

Diligence

Registered area against usable area, the real price behind the payment plan, the developer record, the title category, the guaranteed and unguaranteed parts of the view. The review is written down and the risks are named.

03

Exit

Every purchase is assessed on how it is sold. Who the next buyer is, how deep the resale market is in that specific district, and how long a realistic exit takes in a slow year.
