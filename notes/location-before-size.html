Buy the location before the square meters

Introduction

With one budget you can own two hundred meters in one district or eighty in another. The professional question is not how many meters the money buys.

Discussion

The question is where to place the money so that people are still competing for it in five years. Address carries information that area never will: who wants to live there, what is being built next to it, and how quickly a buyer appears when you decide to sell.

That is why a small unit in a first tier location is often a stronger asset than a large one in a weaker street. Space is easy to price. Demand is not.

Some of the most expensive qualities of a home have no square meters at all.

Key takeaways

  • Price the address before you price the floor plan.
  • Ask who competes for this street in five years, not today.
  • Usable area and registered area are different numbers.
