Chapters

2018

Istanbul

I started where the market is deepest and least forgiving. The first lesson arrived quickly: buyers do not buy when prices are low, sellers do not move when nobody is at the table, and the most rational deal is rarely the one that closes fastest. Learning to read that behaviour mattered more than learning the price lists.

2021

A second market

Working Dubai alongside Istanbul changed the questions. A client with capital in two countries is not choosing between two apartments, they are choosing what happens to the balance of everything they own. That is when advisory stopped being about finding a property and started being about the architecture of a portfolio.

2023

North Cyprus

The third market brought first time overseas buyers, smaller tickets and the highest need for structure. Title category, developer record and the exact wording of a contract decide these outcomes. It is the market where saying no early saves the most money.

Today

Two companies

Şahane in Türkiye and Radolarpro in the United Arab Emirates. Same standard in both: the review is written down, the risks are named, and if the numbers only work when everything goes right, the deal is not ready.
